Agentic Software Development

Trained at the Diffusion.io ↗ agentic development boot camp, I'm leading ABC's shift toward spec-driven agentic engineering—teaching product and development teams how to turn AI tools into reliable delivery workflows.

01 · SPEC FIRST

Clear Intent

Define business constraints, edge cases, and acceptance criteria before an agent writes a line of code.

02 · DELEGATE

Bounded Slices

Break complex features into isolated tasks with crisp ownership instead of throwing giant prompts at a repo.

03 · VERIFY

Independent Checks

Pair agentic speed with adversarial critique, automated tests, and deterministic guardrails before release.

04 · TEACH

Team Adoption

Coach PMs and engineers at ABC through hands-on frameworks so agentic workflows stick across real sprints.
