Financial Data Mart

A financial-data foundation made useful through a reporting product.

I owned the Financial Data Mart product initiative at ABC Fitness—shaping a shared foundation for financial reporting and bringing it to customers through standardized reports. FDM is one of the achievements I’m proudest of from my tenure.

Flagship product initiative · ABC Fitness Ignite Insights

01 · The product challenge

From fragmented data to a consistent reporting experience.

Finance teams need information they can understand and compare across locations. I led FDM to address that product challenge with an enterprise data foundation: connect curated Commerce data to a finance-specific reporting experience inside ABC Ignite Insights.

02 · The product

Build a shared foundation for financial reporting.

FDM organizes curated Commerce data into fact and dimension tables, connecting financial measures—such as revenue and account balances—to context such as location, time and product category. ABC’s public materials describe the data layer and the customer-facing report experience.

  • Finance-specific model: curated financial measures and descriptive dimensions are organized for reporting and analysis.
  • Standardized reporting: a shared source for consistent financial reports across locations and stakeholders.
  • Modern data platform: built on Databricks lakehouse architecture, replacing a legacy data-load process.
  • Customer-facing product: March 2026 release notes describe the initial standardized reports; the help center documents the FDM reporting experience.

03 · My role

Own product direction. Align teams around the customer experience.

I owned FDM as a product—accountable for its direction and the customer problem it solves. I led the initiative from its financial reporting foundation through customer-facing reports, bringing teams across product, data and engineering together around a shared experience.

That is what makes FDM a defining achievement for me: connecting foundational data work to a product finance teams can use. It required enterprise product leadership: holding the customer problem, a strong data foundation and cross-functional delivery in view at the same time.

04 · In production

Financial reports live in ABC Ignite Insights.

ABC’s March 2026 release update describes FDM reports as live, and the help center documents the report catalog and how users access them. The product brings a shared data foundation into a customer-facing experience.

05 · Leadership perspective

Infrastructure becomes product when customers can use it.

FDM turns a shared data architecture into a financial reporting experience customers can use. I focus on aligning the customer problem, the data structure and product direction, then leading teams through delivery. Strong platforms matter when their value is visible in the experience they enable.
