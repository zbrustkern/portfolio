Financial Data Mart

A shared, structured foundation for financial reporting.

I led the product initiative behind Financial Data Mart, one of the achievements I’m proudest of from my tenure at ABC Fitness. This case study focuses on the product and its publicly documented capabilities. The platform and customer-facing reports reflect collaborative team delivery.

Public product documentation · FDM reports described as live in March 2026

01 · The product challenge

Make financial reporting easier to trust and compare.

ABC’s public product overview describes finance teams working across fragmented systems, legacy dependencies and inconsistent reporting. Financial Data Mart (FDM) addresses that foundation problem with a finance-specific reporting layer inside ABC Ignite Insights. The product challenge is both technical and human: give financial stakeholders a consistent structure they can use to understand the business.

02 · The product

Standardize the foundation beneath the reports.

ABC’s published material describes FDM as a centralized, structured financial reporting layer. Its help documentation says it organizes curated Commerce data into fact and dimension tables, separating measures such as revenue and account balances from context such as location, time and product category.

  • Finance-specific structure: financial measures and descriptive dimensions are organized for financial reporting and analysis.
  • A shared reporting foundation: ABC describes FDM as a source for standardized financial reporting across locations and stakeholders.
  • A modern data platform: ABC’s public materials describe FDM as built on Databricks lakehouse architecture and replacing a legacy data-load process.
  • Customer-facing reports: ABC’s March 2026 product update says the initial release includes standardized financial reports; the help center documents the FDM report experience.

03 · My role

Lead the product initiative; deliver with the team.

I led the product initiative behind FDM. It is one of the achievements I’m proudest of from my ABC Fitness tenure. The public feature set is a team outcome; I’m describing my product leadership, not claiming sole engineering, data modeling or architecture authorship.

Foundational data work matters when it becomes a product people can understand and use. FDM brings the underlying structure and reporting experience together around a clear product purpose: more consistent financial information for the people who need to work with it.

04 · Public evidence and maturity

Documented as a live product, without private outcome claims.

ABC’s March 2026 release update describes FDM reports as live. Its help center documents how users find FDM reports and lists the initial report catalog. These public references establish a documented customer-facing product and report experience. I’m not publishing private adoption, performance, revenue or customer results.

05 · Leadership perspective

Infrastructure becomes product when it changes the experience.

A shared financial data layer is not valuable just because it is modern. Its product value comes from making reports more consistent and the information easier for finance teams to use. Leading an initiative like FDM means holding that customer purpose alongside the work of building a strong foundation with a broader team.
