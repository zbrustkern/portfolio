Case study · Personal venture

SendMyNotes

A custom greeting card, written with a real pen and sent with care.

SendMyNotes brings the pleasure of a personal card into a simpler flow. The sender chooses the design and writes the note; a real pen writes it inside before postal fulfillment. Aster & Blanche is the card’s recipient-facing stationery identity.

Live product · Paid-channel experiment

Back to selected ventures Read the DocketStamp case study →

01 · The problem

Keep the gesture. Lose the errands.

A physical card can feel more considered than a quick message, but sending one takes a string of small steps: find a design, write the note, address it, add postage and get it to the mailbox. SendMyNotes makes that process simpler while keeping the message and the paper gesture personal.

02 · Product decisions

Design for both sides of the envelope.

  • Keep the message personal. The sender chooses the design and writes the words that go inside the card.
  • Make it tangible. Real pens write the sender’s message inside the card before it enters postal fulfillment.
  • Connect the two brand roles. SendMyNotes makes sending simple; Aster & Blanche gives the recipient-facing stationery its own identity. They are two sides of one card journey.

03 · My role

Own the journey from product promise to the mailbox.

I lead the product across experience, software and operations. I connect the card design, message, checkout and mailing handoff, because the promise only works when the whole journey feels coherent.

04 · Delivery approach

Use agents to move faster; own the product call.

I organize agent work around focused research, implementation and critique. I set the interfaces and quality bar, integrate the actual changes, test the behavior that matters and own the release decision.

  1. Protect the personal touch. Make it easy to send while the sender stays in control of the words.
  2. Connect digital and physical details. Treat design, message, checkout and fulfillment as one product.
  3. Set a release gate around the money path. A discount-flow fix tied the selected checkout total to the payment intent, with regression coverage for price changes and retries before a staged release.

05 · Product in the real world

A real product journey, from design choice to the mailbox.

SendMyNotes connects product design, checkout and physical fulfillment in a single experience: choose a card, write a personal message, then let a real pen carry it onto paper before the card is mailed. A live paid-channel experiment puts that proposition in front of the market.

06 · Product insight

The easiest gesture should still feel personal.

The sender should feel the effort drop away; the recipient should still feel the care. That product tension shaped the card design, writing process and every handoff from checkout to the mailbox.
